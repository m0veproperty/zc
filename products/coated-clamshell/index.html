Coated Clamshell

Technical Specifications

General

  • Base Material: White or Kraft Paper

  • Coating: Bio-based polymer derived from seaweed

  • Basis Weight: 200-300 gsm

  • Thickness: 350–400 μm

Performance

  • Excellent barrier against oils and fats

  • Passes minimum KIT 10 on the plain surface

Printability

• Compatible with digital printing for high-resolution branding

Recyclability

  • Recyclable within standard paper recycling streams

  • Meets industry compliance thresholds

Food Contact

• Complies with FDA & EU 10/2011 regulations for food contact

Compostability

  • Certified home compostable by TUV Austria
